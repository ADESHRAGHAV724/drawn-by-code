# immortal-jellyfish

- **Style:** cinematic-facts (the kit made for this film: `styles/cinematic-facts/cinema.js`)
- **Format:** 9:16 · 1080×1920 · 49 s · 24 fps · YouTube Short, Hindi voice-over (ElevenLabs
  «Jeet», kept in `private/`; `make-voice.sh` adds 0.4 s and pads to 49 s)
- **Subject:** Turritopsis dohrnii, the «immortal jellyfish». Facts: ~4.5 mm across; when old
  or hurt it can turn its cells back and return to the polyp stage, then bud new medusae;
  scientists study its genome to understand ageing (not to make humans immortal — the user's
  first wording was corrected in the script); it is not truly immortal (fish eat it, disease
  kills it).
- **The jellyfish:** painted in code (`turri.js`) after the user's reference photos (private,
  not committed). A first cut used the photos as torn-edge cutouts; the user asked for the
  code-made one.
- **Captions only, no subtitles.**
