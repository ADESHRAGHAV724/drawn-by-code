# octopus

- **Style:** cinematic-facts, cut fast: 22 shots in 57 s (a new visual every 1.5–3 s), each
  cut with a white flash and a zoom punch — the user asked for visuals that change quickly.
- **Format:** 9:16 · 1080×1920 · 57 s · 24 fps · YouTube Short, Hindi voice-over (ElevenLabs
  «Jeet», kept in `private/`; `make-voice.sh` adds 0.4 s, pads to 57 s)
- **The user's asks:** the nerves and the blue blood vessels in detail; when copper is said,
  the copper molecule side by side; little fish swimming; sea plants and shells on the
  bottom. All painted in code (`octo.js`).
- **Facts:** 3 hearts; blue blood (copper-based hemocyanin); one central brain and a large
  nerve centre in every arm, about two-thirds of the neurons in the arms («9 brains?» is the
  popular framing, asked as a question); colour and texture change in a moment; ink to
  escape; no bones (only the beak is hard), so it squeezes through gaps about the size of
  its beak; opens jars, solves puzzles; regrows lost arms.
- **The copper site:** two Cu atoms bridging an O₂ held side-on, each bound by three
  histidine imidazoles (ball-and-stick, turning), labelled HEMOCYANIN.
