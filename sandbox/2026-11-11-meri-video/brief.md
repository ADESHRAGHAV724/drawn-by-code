# meri-video

- **Style:** warli
- **Format:** 16:9 · 10 s · 24 fps
- **Purpose / audience:** a first story test of the Warli style: a vehicle (a motif the kit
  does not have) and an event with cause and effect, not only a loop.
- **One-line idea:** a car speeds down the village road and hits the big tree; the tree
  shakes, the birds fly off, leaves rain down, the dazed driver staggers out with stars round
  his head and the villagers come running.
- **References:** the style's own template (styles/warli/template.js) and skill.
- **On-screen text (literal):** «Dhyan se chalao!» (Hindi in Latin letters: «drive carefully!»)
- **Music / rhythm:** none yet.
- **Must NOT happen:** no blood, no injury beyond a comic daze; no colour beyond paste white
  on the mud wall; nothing slides on smooth curves (figures and the car on twos).

## Shot list

One continuous wall painting, no cuts.

| Time | Shot | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1.4 | Village | The wall paints itself: border, sun, ground line, hut on the left, the big tree on the right with three birds perched in it |
| 1.4–5.0 | The car | A car drives in from the left, wheels turning, exhaust puffs; it speeds up towards the tree |
| 5.0–6.0 | Crash | The bonnet hits the trunk: a burst of paste rays, the wall shakes, the bonnet crumples, the car bounces back; the tree sways, the birds fly off, leaves start to fall |
| 6.0–8.5 | After | Steam rises from the bonnet; the driver staggers out from behind the car with stars circling his head; a man and a woman with a pot run from the hut |
| 8.5–10 | Lesson | The villagers stop and raise their arms; «Dhyan se chalao!» is written on the wall |
