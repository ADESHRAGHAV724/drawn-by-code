## Round 1

**Auto (review.mjs):** no warnings; motion energy peaks at the crash (5–6 s); 17 ms/frame warm.

**By eye (contact sheet + stills at 1920):**
- The three birds perched in the tree were invisible: paste birds over paste leaves merge
  into the canopy. → moved them onto the tips above the leaves.
- Everything sat low and small, with the top half of the wall empty. → car 300 → 380, tree
  330 → 440, people 120 → 145–150, hut 180 → 230.
- Passing the hut, the car and the hut merged into one white shape. → the car is drawn on a
  rim of wall colour (its silhouette stroked and filled in `WALL` first), so it reads in front.
- In the last shot the villager ran into the driver. → villagers stop at 590 / 480; the
  driver stands at ~740.

**Lesson (to style-warli):** paste on paste merges. Anything that passes in front of another
motif is knocked out with a rim of wall colour, and nothing small (birds, stars) is placed over
a filled area.
