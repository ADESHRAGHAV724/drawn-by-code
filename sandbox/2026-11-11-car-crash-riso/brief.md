# car-crash-riso

- **Style:** risograph
- **Format:** 16:9 · 10 s · 24 fps · no sound
- **Purpose / audience:** the user wanted to see how their own content looks in the risograph
  style: the story of `sandbox/2026-11-11-meri-video` (Warli), reprinted in four riso inks.
- **One-line idea:** a pink car speeds down the village road and hits the big tree; birds fly
  off, leaves fall, the dazed driver staggers out, villagers run in; «ध्यान से चलाओ!».
- **Separations:** sky = pink + yellow screens ramping to the horizon; sun = yellow solid +
  pink screen; hills = blue screen; field = yellow solid + blue screen (green); road = paper +
  navy screen; hut = paper walls with yellow screen, roof pink + yellow (red); tree = brown
  trunk (navy screen + pink + yellow), crown yellow + blue solid with a navy screen shade,
  pink fruit on paper; car = pink solid, paper windows with a blue screen, navy wheels; burst
  = yellow knocked out of everything with a pink core; text navy.
- **Must NOT happen:** a fifth ink; light ink printed over dark without a knockout.

## Shot list (the same beats as meri-video)

| Time | Shot | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1.4 | Village | Sun rises, hut and tree print in, birds perched on the crown |
| 1.4–5.0 | The car | The car drives in from the left, speeding up, puffing exhaust |
| 5.0–6.0 | Crash | Yellow burst, the print shakes, the bonnet crumples, the car bounces back; the tree sways, birds fly off, leaves fall |
| 6.0–8.5 | After | Steam from the bonnet; the driver staggers out with stars round his head; two villagers run in |
| 8.5–10 | Lesson | They raise their arms; «ध्यान से चलाओ!» prints in navy |
