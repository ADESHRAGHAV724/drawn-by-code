# yt-banner

- **Style:** comic (full colour)
- **Format:** a still, 16:9: 1024×576 as asked, and 2560×1440 for upload (YouTube needs at
  least 2048×1152).
- **Purpose / audience:** the user's YouTube channel banner (story animation for a Hindi-speaking
  audience). No channel name on it (the user's choice).
- **Idea:** the user asked for a mix of a story, Spider-Man, a duck and animation. Spider-Man
  is a protected character, so nothing of his (suit, colours, web pattern, logo) is used:
  the spider is a little cartoon spider of our own. First round: one original hero
  («Spider-Duck», a masked duck in a cape swinging on a silk line). The user then asked for
  two or three characters instead («dog, man, house»): a smiling house with window eyes, a
  man waving, his dog, a duck with a bow tie, and the spider let down on its silk line, on a
  green hill in front of a sunset city.
- **YouTube safe area:** on a desktop only the middle band shows (1546×423 of 2560×1440 = x
  203–821, y 203–373 of the 1024×576 frame). The whole cast stands inside it.
