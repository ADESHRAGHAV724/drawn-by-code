# golden-frog

- **Style:** paper cutout (the frog itself is a photo collage, see below)
- **Format:** 9:16 · 1080×1920 · 34 s · 24 fps · a YouTube Short for the user's animal-facts
  channel, Hindi voice-over (ElevenLabs «Jeet», kept in `private/`, re-timed by
  `make-voice.sh`: 0.4 s of air, 34 s)
- **Purpose:** education, fast facts, not a story. Hook first.
- **The frog:** the user's three photos (`private/frog-face.jpg`, `frog-leaves.jpg`,
  `frog-side.jpg`, not committed), cut along an outline traced by hand in photo pixels
  (`FROGS` in scene.js) and glued on a torn white paper backing with a pasted shadow, like a
  magazine cutout. Without the photos each frog falls back to a plain paper frog, so the
  scene runs from the committed code alone. The render is NOT committed (it shows the
  photos, whose rights are the user's).
- **Facts (checked):** ~5 cm long; batrachotoxin; one frog carries about 1 mg of toxin,
  commonly put at enough to kill ~10 people (the «20,000» figure is mice, not people);
  the Emberá of Colombia rub dart tips on its back; it lives only in the Pacific coast
  rainforests of Colombia; the poison comes from its wild diet, so captive frogs are not
  toxic.
- **On screen:** a forest in paper (trees with brown trunks and leafy crowns, big leaves,
  vines, ferns, bushes, flowers, grass, fallen leaves); no subtitles, only key words in
  English (the user's round-2 ask) on torn paper strips.
